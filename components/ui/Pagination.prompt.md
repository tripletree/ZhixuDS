Pill pagination for tables and browse grids: 28px circular buttons, tabular numerals, current page on the bone 8% active-tab treatment, hover tints azure. Ellipsis collapses long ranges; double chevrons jump to first/last.

```jsx
<Pagination page={page} pageCount={24} onChange={setPage} />
<Pagination page={p} pageCount={5} showEnds={false} siblings={2} />
```

Pair under a `DataTable` or product grid, right-aligned or centered. For year/month steppers (2026 « ‹ › »), use the same chevron IconButtons inline instead.
